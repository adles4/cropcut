# Cropcut: Sticker Maker

A completely free sticker maker and background remover for iPhone. No ads, no subscription, no account, no watermark. Everything runs on your device.

**Website:** https://cropcut.app  
**App Store:** https://apps.apple.com/app/id6810356642  
**Privacy policy:** https://cropcut.app/privacy.html

## What it does

- **Cut out anything.** Tap *Remove Background* for an instant automatic cutout. If auto-detection grabs the wrong thing, use *Trace*: draw roughly around the subject with a finger and Cropcut snaps the path to the edges. Tidy up with the eraser.
- **Keep stickers, use them anywhere.** Every cutout goes into the sticker tray and is saved to Photos as a transparent PNG, so it works in iMessage, WhatsApp, Notes, or any other app.
- **Compose.** Drop stickers onto any photo, drag, pinch and rotate, add text or freehand markup, then save or share.

## Why it's different

Most sticker and background-remover apps on the App Store are free to download but charge a subscription for the useful parts, show ads, or watermark free exports. Cropcut has none of those. There is no Pro tier. All processing happens on the device; the app has no server and no account, and its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

Compared with the iPhone's built-in Add Sticker feature, Cropcut adds manual tracing with edge snapping, an eraser, PNG export you can use outside Messages, and a Compose canvas for building scenes.

## Availability

iOS, on the App Store in 175 countries. The app's interface is available in 31 languages, including English, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Thai, Vietnamese, Ukrainian and Turkish.
