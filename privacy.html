Cropcut Privacy Policy

Effective 9 September 2026

Cropcut is an iOS app that turns photos into stickers and lets you compose them onto other photos. It is made by one independent developer. The short version: Cropcut does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything the app does happens on your device.

What Cropcut does not do

  • No account, sign-up, or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • No uploading of your photos, stickers, or creations to any server. Cropcut has no server.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, or camera roll beyond the photos you explicitly choose.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone, because there is nothing to sell or share.

Photos you choose

When you pick a photo, Cropcut reads it only to let you cut it out, crop it, or compose with it. When you save a sticker or a creation, it is written to your Photos library on your device. Photos and stickers never leave your device unless you share them using the iOS share sheet, in which case the app or service you share to handles that content under its own privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Your sticker tray and any in-progress work are stored locally in the app's own storage on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes that data. If you use iCloud backup or device backup, Apple may include the app's local data in your backup under Apple's terms; Cropcut has no access to those backups.

Permissions

Cropcut asks for access to your Photos library only so you can pick photos and save results. You can limit this to selected photos or revoke it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

Children

Cropcut does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If Cropcut ever changes in a way that affects your privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Any such change would also be described in the App Store "What's New" notes for that version.
